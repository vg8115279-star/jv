We are JV.INC

JV.inc is a company focused on the development of digital projects and technological solutions across different sectors. We manage and develop three main projects: a gastronomy-related project, a sports items project, and our flagship project, an IT company dedicated to the world of video games and peripherals.

Our company was created with the aim of combining creativity, technology, and functionality to build digital platforms that are clear, modern, and user-friendly. At JV.inc, we believe that every project is an opportunity to grow, learn, and improve, both technically and creatively.

Our goals

  • Create well-structured and intuitive websites.
  • Maintain a consistent visual identity across all projects.
  • Apply modern web standards and good design practices.
  • Continuously improve our technical and creative skills.

What we do

  • Corporate websites.
  • IT projects, including online video game and peripheral stores.
  • Digital platforms with a strong focus on usability and design consistency.

Technologies we use

  • HTML5 for content structure.
  • CSS3 for visual design, layout, and style consistency.
  • Design and planning tools to organize and structure our projects effectively.

OUR PROJECTS

Flavor District

This gastronomic project represents the arrival of a new author-style restaurant in the city of Alicante. The concept is based on research and inspiration drawn from different countries around the world, combining international flavors, techniques, and ingredients to create a unique and innovative menu. Our chefs are constantly gathering culinary influences to design dishes that aim to stimulate the more than 8,000 taste buds of the human tongue, offering a complete sensory experience. This project goes beyond traditional dining, turning gastronomy into an artistic expression where each dish tells a story and evokes emotions..

SPORTS HUB

Sports Hub is our sports items project, dedicated to providing high-quality sports equipment and accessories for athletes of all levels.
